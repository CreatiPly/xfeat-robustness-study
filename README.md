
# XFeat Robustness Study

## Overview
A research project evaluating the robustness and efficiency
of XFeat, SIFT and ORB under image degradation.

## Benchmark Paper
XFeat: Accelerated Features for Lightweight Image Matching
CVPR 2024.

## Research Question
How do Gaussian blur and brightness reduction affect
homography estimation accuracy across XFeat, SIFT and ORB?

## Methods
- XFeat (pretrained)
- SIFT
- ORB

## Dataset
HPatches full image sequences.

## Experimental Conditions
- Clean images
- Gaussian blur (sigma 1 and 2)
- Brightness multipliers (0.7 and 0.4)
- Combined blur and brightness degradation

## Evaluation
- Mean Homography Accuracy
- Match count
- Inlier ratio
- Runtime
